Booking terms

This page is a structural placeholder for testing the booking flow. It must be completed and approved by the accommodation provider before reservations are enabled.

1. Contracting parties and property

Add the legal identity, address, registration details and exact accommodation description.

2. Price and included services

The booking summary must show the current accommodation price, mandatory charges, included services, tourist tax and any refundable security deposit.

3. Reservation status and payment

For bank-transfer bookings, define the hold period, payment instructions, when the reservation becomes confirmed and what happens if payment is late.

4. Cancellation and changes

The rate-specific cancellation and prepayment policy shown in the booking flow forms part of the accepted booking terms.

5. Arrival, departure and house rules

Add confirmed check-in/check-out times, occupancy, pets, smoking, events, noise, damage and identification rules.

6. Complaints, governing law and contact

Add the applicable Croatian consumer information, complaint procedure, governing law and official contact details.
